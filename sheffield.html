Good to know

What should my child bring?Nothing. All materials and aprons are provided. Clothes that can get a little messy are best.
What if we miss a class?Let us know and, where there is space, your child can catch up at our Worksop class the following Wednesday.
How do payments work?Ten equal monthly payments from September to June, taken automatically. Please give four weeks' notice before a new term if you wish to leave.
Will I see my child's work?Yes. You get a photo after every class, and finished pieces come home at the end of each half term.
