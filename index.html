What happens in a lesson

Every class follows the same five steps, so children know what to expect and settle quickly.

  1. 1Warm upA three-minute drawing of the day to get hands moving.
  2. 2Live demoThe technique or artist of the week, shown live at the table.
  3. 3CreateStep-by-step making, with one-to-one help around the room.
  4. 4Tidy upChildren wash brushes and look after their own space.
  5. 5Gallery walkEveryone shares one thing they are proud of. Parents get a photo the same day.

A full year of art, planned

Six themes, one for each half term. Each runs for six weeks and ends with a finished piece and a mini exhibition.

Only at Sandstone

Every year ends with a sand animation of their own

In the last half term, children learn the live sand animation techniques our founder performs on stage. They plan a short story, draw it in sand on a light table and film it. The films are premiered at our end-of-year exhibition, followed by a live sand performance for families.

Meet your teacher

Hello, I'm Bill.

I'm Bilal Köse, a professional live sand animation artist. I have performed at Harrods, Soho Theatre, the University of Oxford and Newcastle Cathedral, and I have taught drawing and painting to children for ten years.

I started Sandstone because I believe every child can learn to draw well when it is taught one clear step at a time. My classes are calm, structured and fun, and children leave each term with work they are genuinely proud of.
